The beauty of our icons, the chanting of the choir, and the fragrant incense rising upward all
remind us that heaven has drawn near to this world through the liturgical life of the Church.

Our parish choir records selections from the divine services throughout the church year. Recordings
are published to our [YouTube channel]({{ site.social.youtube }}).
