Our parish maintains a well-stocked bookstore where parishioners and visitors can find many
excellent works about the Orthodox faith, ranging from Church history, liturgics, and theology, to
the lives of the saints, daily readings, children's books, and practical guides for everyday
Orthodox life. We also carry a large selection of prayer books, icons, candles, and prayer ropes for
personal devotion and prayer in one's home chapel.

Our books are selected from many fine Orthodox publishing houses, including
[Ancient Faith Publishing](https://www.ancientfaith.com/publishing),
[Saint Vladimir's Seminary Press](https://svspress.com/),
[Saint Tikhon's Monastery Press](https://stmpress.com/), and
[Holy Transfiguration Monastery](https://thehtm.org/). Our icons come from
[Legacy Icons](https://legacyicons.com/) and [Damascene Gallery](https://damascenegallery.com/).
Many of our prayer ropes are handmade on Kodiak Island, Alaska.

## Seasonal reading

Among our book selection, we stock many classic books that provide inspirational reading for the
major liturgical seasons of the church year, including Great Lent and the Nativity Fast:

- _Great Lent &mdash; Journey to Pascha_, by Fr. Alexander Schmemann
- _Thirty Steps to Heaven_, by Archimandrite Vassilios Papavassiliou
- _The Lenten Spring_, by Fr. Thomas Hopko
- _The Ladder of Divine Ascent_, by St. John Climacus
- _The Winter Pascha_, by Fr. Thomas Hopko
- _Meditations for Advent_, by Fr. Vassilios Papavassiliou

New arrivals to our bookstore are regularly announced in our newsletter&mdash;check in often to see
the latest additions.
