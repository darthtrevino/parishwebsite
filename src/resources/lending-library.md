In addition to our [bookstore](/resources/bookstore/), our parish maintains a large lending library
for parishioners and visitors to check out books on the Orthodox faith. Located in the parish hall,
our library contains more than 700 titles, including rare and out-of-print books that are difficult
to find other than in our library.

We are cataloguing our lending library online at LibraryThing.
