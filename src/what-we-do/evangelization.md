Saint Elizabeth was planted as a mission in 2001 and became the first Orthodox parish of Kitsap
County. That missionary character has never left us. Many of our members are converts, and our
parish life is shaped by the ongoing work of welcoming inquirers, catechizing them, and receiving
them into the Church.

Our evangelization takes ordinary forms:

- **Open doors.** Every service is open to visitors, with greeters ready to help newcomers get
  situated. See our [Visiting](/come-and-see/visiting/) page.
- **Teaching.** Our _Orthodox Conversations_ class is designed for inquirers from non-Orthodox,
  non-Christian, and non-religious backgrounds. See [Learning](/come-and-see/learning/).
- **Public lectures.** We sponsor talks in our local Sons of Norway building on Orthodox history,
  theology, and culture.
- **Presence in our town.** We take part in Poulsbo's annual Viking Fest and the Kitsap County Fair.
- **Broadcast and recording.** Our homilies, talks, and liturgical music are shared through our
  [YouTube channel]({{ site.social.youtube }}) and our
  [liturgical chant](/resources/liturgical-chant/) archive.

on a Sunday at 10:00 a.m., or to call Father John at [{{ site.phone }}](tel:{{ site.phoneHref }}).
