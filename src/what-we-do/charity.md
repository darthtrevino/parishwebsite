Our patron, [Holy New Martyr Elizabeth](/who-we-are/our-saint/), gave away the wealth of a princess
and spent her nights at the bedsides of the poor. Her example places charity near the center of our
common life.

Parishioners of Saint Elizabeth have contributed to local Habitat for Humanity builds, rung the bell
for charitable collections during the Nativity season, and supported Orthodox mission work abroad,
including in Guatemala. Our parish charity fund provides direct, discreet assistance to neighbors in
need on the Kitsap Peninsula, administered by the rector.

If you or someone you know is in need, please contact Father John at
[{{ site.phone }}](tel:{{ site.phoneHref }}) or [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

If you would like to support this work, you can [give to the parish charity fund](/giving/).
