This knowing is not what many in the modern world understand knowledge to be, limited to book
learning and resulting from rationalistic argumentation. It is sacramental.

Saint Elizabeth Church welcomes all who are drawn to the One True God known in holy Orthodoxy. Our
parish consists of many converts to the faith, and we provide a welcoming atmosphere to those
desiring to learn more about it. This can be done through the simple act of attending our liturgical
services, for it is in divine worship that the fullest experience of God occurs.

For those who desire instruction about the faith, we have several classes as well.

## Orthodox Conversations

Our *Orthodox Conversations* class is designed for "inquirers": people from non-Orthodox,
non-Christian, or even non-religious backgrounds who are interested in learning about our faith. This
class&mdash;consisting of topical conversations led by Father John&mdash;meets **Wednesday evenings at
7:30 p.m.** after vespers. It is attended by many of our parishioners.

## Catechesis

Our *Catechesis* class is designed for our catechumens&mdash;those who, after prayer and discernment,
have resolved to become Orthodox Christians and require a wide exploration of the faith in its
entirety. Our catechism class surveys the entire range of learning that prepares one for sacramental
reception into the Church. It meets **Monday evenings at 6:30 p.m.**, and also during the second half
of the Sunday Divine Liturgy. Catechesis typically takes a break following Pascha in the spring until
a new annual cycle begins in September.

## Reading Fellowship

Our parish also hosts a reading fellowship that explores the Scriptures and various books written by
Orthodox authors. It provides a good opportunity for discussion and fellowship. Many of the books
discussed are available in our [bookstore](/resources/bookstore/) and
[lending library](/resources/lending-library/).

## Online resources

For those who would like to learn about the Orthodox faith online, we strongly recommend exploring
the vast content of [Ancient Faith Radio](https://www.ancientfaith.com/), which offers many podcasts,
blogs, and special presentations designed for inquirers. Their
[streaming music channel](https://www.ancientfaith.com/radio/music) offers a 24/7 window into the
riches of Orthodox liturgical music.

The [Orthodox Arts Journal](https://orthodoxartsjournal.org/) has a wealth of information for learning
about Orthodox liturgical arts more broadly, including iconography.

Finally, the [Orthodox Church in America]({{ site.jurisdiction.url }}) has a wonderful
[question &amp; answer page](https://www.oca.org/questions), and hosts online all four volumes of
[*The Orthodox Faith*](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith) by Father Thomas Hopko (of
blessed memory), a classic and comprehensive overview of the Orthodox faith.
