We value every person God brings through our doors, whether as a visitor or a new member.

First-time visitors are encouraged to contact Father John in advance by phone or email if they wish
to learn about our parish before attending. This can be particularly helpful for those who have not
attended a Divine Liturgy before. You can reach him at
[{{ site.phone }}](tel:{{ site.phoneHref }}) or [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

Alternatively, we have a group of parishioners assigned to greet visitors when they arrive and help
them get situated. If you would like a service book, or information about our parish, or about
Orthodoxy generally, they are happy to help. And Father John may come out of the altar to introduce
himself and say a word of welcome as well.

First-time visitors may also like to read a little in advance about
[Orthodox worship and decorum](/what-we-do/worship/) within a typical temple such as ours.

## After the Liturgy

On Sundays, the Divine Liturgy is followed by a coffee hour and pot-luck meal. Visitors are warmly
invited to stay for this happy time of conversation and fellowship, which for some parishioners can
last until mid-afternoon. During this time our children attend a half-hour church school, and
visitors are welcome to have their children attend as well. Children can also enjoy our fenced
playground (or indoor play area) while parents have time to relax, talk, and peruse our
[bookstore](/resources/bookstore/).

## Staying in touch

Those who would like to leave contact information can do so by signing our guest registry at the back
of the church, or by asking one of our greeters for a form to fill out. Father John will then be able
to contact you and, if you would like, place your name on our mailing list.
