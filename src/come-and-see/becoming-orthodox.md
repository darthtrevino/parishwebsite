We believe that every human being is made in the image of God and possesses the same human nature
assumed by our Lord Jesus Christ in the incarnation.

Visitors to this website and to our church will find themselves in different stages of interest in
Orthodoxy, and we have some who attend our church on a regular basis but have (still) not become
Orthodox for a variety of reasons. We believe our calling is simply to be the Church to everyone who
encounters us, and to offer a joyful and faithful parish home for them.

## The path to reception

For those who decide to become Orthodox, we have an established process for sacramental reception.

**1. Inquirer.** Typically, a person, a couple, or a family will begin attending the services and
speaking with the pastor about their interest. They will be encouraged to attend one of the
[courses of learning](/come-and-see/learning/) we offer, and to become acquainted with some of our
parishioners.

**2. Catechumen.** If and when they reach the conclusion that they desire to become members of the
Orthodox Church, they will then be received as catechumens. This stage of the journey into Orthodoxy
can last several months to a year, depending upon prior Christian formation and knowledge about the
Orthodox faith.

**3. Reception.** Finally, when the time appointed for their reception arrives&mdash;for many this
will be the glorious feast of Pascha&mdash;they will be sacramentally received. At that point, their
journey into Orthodoxy ends, and their journey into the Kingdom of Heaven begins.
